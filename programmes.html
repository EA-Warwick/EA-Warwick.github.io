Programmes

Structured learning for deeper impact

Each programme runs for 8 weeks, starting from Week 3 of term. All are free and student-led. No prior knowledge of EA is needed.

📖 Introductory Fellowship
8 weeks +

Learn and discuss EA ideas with a small group of like-minded students. The Intro Fellowship is a free 4-week programme with weekly readings and discussions exploring the most pressing challenges of our time — and how to act on them.

Fellows join a campus community of dedicated altruists and gain access to socials, co-working space, and a global network of people committed to doing as much good as possible. The application deadline for this programme is Sunday, 11th October.

Apply
🏛️ Arête Fellowship
8 weeks +

Designed for students ready to commit to starting an altruistic career and looking to engage in a selective, structured program based on EA’s core principles. Similar to the Introductory Fellowship, this is an 8-week programme with weekly readings and facilitated discussions exploring the world’s most pressing challenges—with a direct emphasis on practical outcomes and career execution.

The application deadline for this programme is Sunday, 11th October. This programme runs alongside the Introductory Fellowship.

Apply
🤖 AI Intro Fellowship
7 weeks · with Warwick AI +

Co-hosted with Warwick AI, this 7-week fellowship is your entry point to a career in AI safety, ethics, and governance. Choose between a technical or governance track, with weekly readings and small-group discussions.

After building the foundations, you'll have the opportunity to develop a project to launch your work in AI safety. The application deadline for this programme is Friday, 16th October.

Apply
🌱 Alternative Proteins Fellowship
8 weeks · with Alternative Protein Project Warwick +

Explore solutions to some of the most pressing challenges of our time — from food insecurity to environmental degradation and animal suffering. The fellowship examines plant-based foods, fermentation technologies, and cultivated meat.

A free, student-led programme combining self-paced learning with weekly sessions, running from Week 3 to Week 10. The application deadline for this programme is Friday, 9th October.

Apply
🧬 Biosecurity Reading Group
8 weeks +

Discover the Biosecurity Cause Area with other absolute beginners. Learn about preventing catastrophic or engineered pandemics, especially in the context of emerging technologies, and how to respond to these risks. no experience required and all backgrounds welcome.

Apply
✍️ Effective Thesis Accelerator
8 weeks +

Designed to take you from an initial thesis or research idea to a fully developed, high-impact proposal with a clear Theory of Change. Combines self-directed modules and worksheets with weekly group discussions.

You'll explore what makes research high-impact, map critical global problems, and identify clear pathways to change. Facilitators offer 1-1 feedback on your projects throughout. The application deadline for this programme is Sunday, 11th October.

Apply Learn More
📜 Effective Project Fellowship
8 weeks +

Learn how to do high-impact research and produce a small piece of work that can support applications to prestigious programmes like ERA Cambridge. The fellowship walks you through problem selection, finding the bottleneck, developing a Theory of Change, and executing the project.

The application deadline for this programme is Sunday, 11th October. This programme runs alongside the Effective Thesis Accelerator.
